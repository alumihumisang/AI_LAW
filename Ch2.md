# Chapter 2 Research Background

## 2. Background

This chapter first reviews the domestic and international studies related to the proposed Severity-Aware Dual-Knowledge-Graph (SDKG) scheme. Section 2.1 presents the related works, and Section 2.2 discusses the research motivation of the proposed SDKG scheme.

本章首先回顧與所提出之 Severity-Aware Dual-Knowledge-Graph（SDKG）scheme 相關之國內外研究成果。其中，2.1 節介紹相關研究，2.2 節則說明所提出 SDKG scheme 之研究動機。

## 2.1 Related Works

Existing studies related to the proposed SDKG scheme can be organized into three closely connected directions. The first direction concerns RAG and extended retrieval-generation strategies, which provide external references for large language models. The second direction concerns knowledge-graph-enhanced retrieval and graph representation methods, including TAARN, which is considered as the main competing method. The third direction concerns legal-domain LLM applications, especially the difficulty of using LLMs to generate legally reliable documents. These directions jointly explain why traffic-accident civil complaint generation requires a retrieval structure that can provide both external grounding and task-specific legal organization.

與所提出 SDKG scheme 相關之既有研究可整理為三個緊密相關的方向。第一個方向為 RAG 及其延伸檢索生成策略，用以為大型語言模型提供外部參考資料。第二個方向為結合知識圖譜之檢索方法與圖譜表示方法，其中包含作為主要對手方法之 TAARN。第三個方向則為大型語言模型在法律領域中的應用，特別是法律文件生成之可靠性問題。上述研究共同說明，交通事故民事起訴書生成需要一種同時提供外部依據與任務特定法律組織能力的檢索結構。

Retrieval-Augmented Generation has become an important technique for reducing hallucination and improving factual grounding in knowledge-intensive text generation. Lewis et al. [1] first formulated RAG by combining document retrieval with sequence generation, allowing the model to condition its output on retrieved external documents. Gao et al. [3] reviewed RAG systems from a system-level perspective, including retrieval strategy, document chunking, reranking, and prompt design. Karpukhin et al. [4] proposed Dense Passage Retrieval, showing that dense vector retrieval can improve open-domain question answering by retrieving semantically relevant passages. Izacard and Grave [5] further proposed Fusion-in-Decoder, demonstrating that multiple retrieved passages can be integrated during generation. These studies establish RAG as a practical framework for grounding generated text in external evidence.

檢索增強生成已成為降低大型語言模型幻覺並提升事實依據的重要技術。Lewis 等人 [1] 首先系統化提出 RAG 架構，將文件檢索與序列生成結合，使模型能根據外部檢索文件生成回答。Gao 等人 [3] 從系統層面回顧 RAG，涵蓋檢索策略、文件切分、重排序與 prompt 設計等議題。Karpukhin 等人 [4] 提出 Dense Passage Retrieval，說明密集向量檢索可透過語意相關段落改善開放領域問答。Izacard 與 Grave [5] 進一步提出 Fusion-in-Decoder，證明多個檢索段落可於生成階段被整合使用。上述研究奠定 RAG 作為外部證據支撐生成內容之重要基礎。

To improve the structural capacity of retrieval-generation systems, recent studies have integrated knowledge graphs into RAG. Zhu et al. [10] introduced a KG-guided retrieval approach that uses entity relations and semantic paths to improve document selection. Edge et al. [11] proposed GraphRAG, which constructs graph-based communities and uses local-to-global graph information for query-focused summarization. Peng et al. [12] surveyed graph retrieval-augmented generation and summarized the use of graph structures for retrieval, reasoning, and generation. Han et al. [13] further discussed retrieval-augmented generation with graphs, showing that graphs can help represent relational knowledge beyond independent text chunks. Sun et al. [14] proposed KERAG to enhance retrieval with knowledge guidance, while Wu et al. [15] proposed query-centric graph retrieval augmented generation. Yu et al. [16] also observed that KG-based RAG must carefully control whether the retrieved graph information really matches the user's need. These studies show that graph structure can improve retrieval, but also that graph design must be aligned with the downstream task.

為提升檢索生成系統的結構能力，近年研究開始將知識圖譜納入 RAG。Zhu 等人 [10] 提出 KG-guided retrieval，利用實體關係與語義路徑改善文件選取。Edge 等人 [11] 提出 GraphRAG，透過圖上社群與由局部至整體的圖資訊支援 query-focused summarization。Peng 等人 [12] 回顧 graph retrieval-augmented generation，整理圖結構於檢索、推理與生成中的使用方式。Han 等人 [13] 進一步討論 retrieval-augmented generation with graphs，指出圖結構可表示超越獨立文字片段的關係知識。Sun 等人 [14] 提出 KERAG 以知識導引強化檢索，Wu 等人 [15] 則提出 query-centric graph retrieval augmented generation。Yu 等人 [16] 亦指出，KG-based RAG 必須謹慎控制被檢索之圖資訊是否真正符合使用者需求。上述研究顯示，圖結構可改善檢索，但圖設計亦必須與下游任務對齊。

Graph representation studies provide another foundation for graph-enhanced retrieval. Graph Attention Networks allow a model to assign different weights to different neighboring nodes [17]. GraphReach further studies graph reachability and position-aware graph representation, showing that graph structures can support reasoning about reachable nodes and relational positions [18]. In legal-domain retrieval, graph-based methods have also been applied to legal norms and legal cases. De Martim [19] proposed a Graph RAG approach for legal norms with hierarchical and temporal structures. LEXA uses graph contrastive learning with contextualized LLM embeddings for legal case retrieval [20]. Barron et al. [21] combined vector stores, knowledge graphs, and hierarchical non-negative matrix factorization for legal retrieval-augmented generation. CaseGNN++ further applies graph contrastive learning and graph augmentation to legal case retrieval [22]. These studies indicate that legal retrieval benefits from graph structure, but also requires the graph to reflect the legal purpose of the task.

圖譜表示研究亦構成結合圖譜之檢索方法的重要基礎。Graph Attention Networks 使模型能對不同鄰近節點給予不同權重 [17]。GraphReach 進一步研究 graph reachability 與 position-aware graph representation，說明圖結構可支援可達節點與關係位置之推理 [18]。在法律領域檢索中，圖結構亦已被應用於法律規範與法律案例。De Martim [19] 針對 legal norms 提出具有階層與時間結構之 Graph RAG 方法。LEXA 使用 graph contrastive learning 與 contextualized LLM embeddings 進行法律案例檢索 [20]。Barron 等人 [21] 結合 vector stores、knowledge graphs 與 hierarchical non-negative matrix factorization 進行法律 RAG。CaseGNN++ 則將 graph contrastive learning 與 graph augmentation 應用於法律案例檢索 [22]。上述研究顯示，法律檢索可受益於圖結構，但圖本身仍須反映任務的法律目的。

Among graph-based approaches, TAARN is particularly relevant because it combines graph relations, text descriptions, path extraction, and graph/text attention [2]. In the original TAARN setting, a knowledge graph is represented through entities, relations, and triples, and the model jointly learns structural and textual information. In the adaptation considered here, each legal case can be treated as a complete case node, and edges can be constructed according to shared case features such as common laws, compensation items, injury types, or accident characteristics. Random-walk-style path extraction can then obtain case-level paths from the graph, while graph/text attention can learn which graph connections and case descriptions are more useful for retrieving relevant cases. The strength of this design is that it goes beyond pure semantic retrieval and exploits both case text and graph relations. It therefore provides a stronger comparison target than conventional semantic retrieval.

在圖譜式方法中，TAARN 特別相關，因為其結合圖上關係、文字描述、path extraction，以及 graph/text attention [2]。在原始 TAARN 設定中，知識圖譜係由 entities、relations 與 triples 表示，模型同時學習結構資訊與文字資訊。在法律案例檢索之改編情境中，每一筆法律案件可被視為完整案件節點，而案件節點之間可依共同特徵建立關係，例如共同法條、共同賠償項目、共同傷勢類型或共同事故特徵。接著，random-walk-style path extraction 可從圖上取得案件層級路徑，而 graph/text attention 則可學習哪些圖上連結與案件文字對檢索相關案例較有幫助。此方法的優點在於，其並非單純依賴語意相似度，而是能同時利用案件文字與圖上關係。因此，其可作為比傳統語意檢索更強的比較對象。

More specifically, the purpose of TAARN is to reduce the gap between symbolic graph structure and textual semantic representation. A graph-only representation may preserve relations but lose the textual descriptions that explain the meaning of entities and relations, while a text-only representation may capture language similarity but ignore long-term relational dependencies. TAARN addresses this issue by combining text-augmented node or relation representations with path-based graph reasoning. Its attention mechanism further allows the model to emphasize more informative graph paths or textual descriptions during representation learning. When adapted to traffic-accident case retrieval, this means that TAARN can retrieve cases not only because they are textually similar, but also because they are connected through graph paths derived from shared legal or factual features.

更具體而言，TAARN 之目的在於降低符號式圖結構與文字語意表示之間的落差。單純圖表示雖可保留關係，但可能遺失說明 entities 與 relations 意義的文字資訊；單純文字表示雖可捕捉語言相似度，卻可能忽略長期關係依賴。TAARN 透過 text-augmented node 或 relation representations 結合 path-based graph reasoning，以處理上述問題。其 attention mechanism 亦使模型能在 representation learning 過程中強調較有資訊量的圖上路徑或文字描述。若改編至交通事故案例檢索，這代表 TAARN 不只會因文字相似而檢索案例，也能透過由共同法律或事實特徵形成之 graph paths 找到相關案例。

For this reason, TAARN should be treated as a meaningful competing method rather than a simple semantic-retrieval baseline. Its advantage is that it provides a graph/text relevance model that is stronger than conventional top-\(k\) semantic retrieval. It can represent multi-hop dependencies, use random-walk-style paths to expand beyond direct neighbors, and combine case descriptions with graph connectivity. In the context of complaint generation, these characteristics make TAARN a strong comparison target because it can retrieve generally related cases through both text and graph structure. However, TAARN is not designed to impose an explicit severity order on the retrieved cases. It can identify relevant cases, but it does not directly distinguish whether a retrieved case should be used as a lighter-side reference or a heavier-side reference for drafting compensation and injury arguments.

因此，TAARN 應被視為有意義的對手方法，而非單純語意檢索 baseline。其優點在於提供比傳統 top-\(k\) 語意檢索更強的 graph/text relevance model。它可以表示 multi-hop dependencies，透過 random-walk-style paths 擴展至直接鄰居以外的節點，並結合案件描述與圖上連結。在起訴書生成情境中，這些特性使 TAARN 成為較強的比較對象，因為其能透過文字與圖結構取得一般相關案例。然而，TAARN 並非為檢索案例施加明確嚴重度排序而設計。它可以辨識 relevant cases，但不會直接區分檢索案例應作為較輕側參考或較重側參考，以支援賠償與傷勢論述之撰寫。

Legal-domain LLM research further shows why structured retrieval is important. Hou et al. [6] and Dehghani et al. [7] reviewed recent LLM-based legal AI systems and observed that legal-domain generation still faces challenges in hallucination, legal grounding, and domain adaptation. Guha et al. [8] introduced LegalBench to evaluate legal tasks beyond general language ability. Jiang et al. [9] attempted to reduce hallucination through contrastive learning with hallucinated outputs as negative samples. These studies indicate that legal generation needs not only fluent language output, but also reliable external references and task-specific structural control.

法律領域大型語言模型研究亦進一步說明結構化檢索的重要性。Hou 等人 [6] 與 Dehghani 等人 [7] 回顧近期 LLM-based legal AI 系統，指出法律生成仍面臨幻覺、法律依據不穩定與領域適配等挑戰。Guha 等人 [8] 提出 LegalBench，說明法律任務需要超越一般語言能力的專門化評估。Jiang 等人 [9] 嘗試透過將幻覺輸出作為負樣本的對比式學習降低錯誤。上述研究顯示，法律生成不只需要流暢文字，也需要可靠外部參考與符合任務需求的結構控制。

Based on the above literature, conventional semantic retrieval still has several limitations for traffic-accident civil complaint generation. Most RAG pipelines retrieve documents mainly according to semantic similarity between the query and candidate texts. This is useful for finding textually related materials, but it does not guarantee that the retrieved cases are legally suitable as drafting references. A traffic-accident complaint requires the generated text to preserve accident facts, injury descriptions, compensation items, party relations, and legal claims in a stable structure. If retrieved cases are treated merely as top-\(k\) similar texts, the generation model may copy irrelevant facts, borrow unsuitable monetary amounts, or lose the logical relation between injury and compensation.

基於上述文獻，傳統語意檢索在交通事故民事起訴書生成上仍有若干限制。多數 RAG 流程主要依 query 與候選文本之語意相似度取得文件。此方法雖有助於找到文字上相關的資料，但不保證檢索案例在法律結構上適合作為撰寫參考。交通事故起訴書要求生成結果穩定保留事故事實、傷勢描述、賠償項目、當事人關係與法律請求。若檢索案例只是被視為 top-\(k\) 相似文本，模型可能複製不相關事實、誤借不適合的金額，或無法維持傷勢與賠償之間的邏輯連結。

The problem is not only retrieval accuracy, but also the lack of legal structure in the retrieval target. In traffic-accident civil disputes, two cases may use similar words but differ substantially in injury severity, compensation scope, liability relation, or evidentiary context. Conversely, two cases may use different surface expressions but still share a similar legal structure. Conventional semantic retrieval has difficulty distinguishing these situations because vector retrieval primarily operates on textual proximity. Therefore, RAG is a necessary foundation, but it is not sufficient for high-quality traffic-accident civil complaint generation.

此問題不只是檢索準確率不足，而是檢索目標缺乏法律結構。於交通事故民事爭議中，兩個案件可能用語相似，但在傷勢嚴重度、賠償範圍、責任關係或證據脈絡上有明顯差異。相反地，兩個案件也可能表面用語不同，卻具有相近的法律結構。傳統語意檢索主要依靠文本接近程度，因此較難區分上述情形。因此，RAG 是必要基礎，但仍不足以單獨完成高品質交通事故民事起訴書生成。

TAARN-style graph/text retrieval addresses part of this problem by using both graph relations and case text. However, its limitation is also clear for the present task. A generic case graph mainly captures whether cases are related through shared features, but it does not explicitly represent whether a neighboring case is lighter or heavier than the query case. For example, two cases may share the same compensation item or injury label, but their severity and suitable compensation reference may be very different. In other words, TAARN-style retrieval can help find related cases, but it does not directly organize retrieved references according to severity direction or complaint-drafting structure.

TAARN-style graph/text retrieval 透過同時使用圖上關係與案件文字，部分處理了上述問題。然而，其限制對本任務而言仍相當明確。一般案件圖主要捕捉案件是否因共同特徵而相關，卻沒有明確表示鄰近案例相對於 query 是較輕或較重。例如，兩個案件可能具有相同賠償項目或傷勢標籤，但實際嚴重度與適合參考之賠償範圍可能差異很大。換言之，TAARN-style retrieval 能協助尋找相關案件，但未直接依嚴重度方向或書狀撰寫結構組織檢索參考。

This limitation is especially important in the AccidentClaim setting. Directly obtaining complete civil complaints from claimants or law firms is difficult. Therefore, the complaint-style case database is based on data constructed from publicly available Taiwanese traffic-accident civil judgments, which were cleaned and reconstructed with the assistance of a large language model into complaint-style case materials. Since many traffic-accident judgments in Taiwan are handled through simplified procedures, their written reasons are often concise. When complaint-style materials are reconstructed from such judgments, the resulting text may be incomplete, overly brief, or unstable in legal structure. This makes it more important for retrieval to provide organized references rather than merely returning similar texts.

此限制在 AccidentClaim 情境中特別重要。由於直接從請求人或律師事務所取得完整民事起訴書並不容易，故起訴書樣式案件資料庫係由公開交通事故民事判決書清洗與重建而成，並經由大型語言模型輔助整理為近似起訴書格式之案件資料。由於臺灣多數交通事故案件於實務上常以簡易程序處理，判決理由文字通常較為精簡，當此類判決被重建為起訴書樣式材料時，所得文本可能不完整、過度簡略，或在法律結構上不穩定。因此，檢索階段更需要提供經過組織的參考，而不能只是回傳語意相似文本。

Overall, the literature shows a clear progression. RAG can improve factual grounding, but it often retrieves flat similar texts. Knowledge-graph-enhanced methods can introduce relations and structural information, but generic graph relations may not match the needs of traffic-accident complaint drafting. TAARN-style graph/text retrieval provides a strong competing method by combining case graph paths and case text, yet it still lacks explicit severity direction. Therefore, the proposed SDKG scheme focuses on using complete cases as nodes, constructing severity-ordered DKG configurations, and retrieving references through the light-heavy tree and heavy-light tree.

整體而言，既有研究呈現出清楚的發展脈絡。RAG 可提升生成內容的事實依據，但多半取得的是平面的相似文本。結合知識圖譜的方法可引入關係與結構資訊，但通用圖關係未必符合交通事故起訴書撰寫需求。TAARN-style graph/text retrieval 透過案件圖路徑與案件文字結合，提供了較強的對手方法，但仍缺乏明確的嚴重度方向。因此，所提出之 SDKG scheme 聚焦於以完整案件作為節點，建立 severity-ordered DKG configurations，並透過 light-heavy tree 與 heavy-light tree 取得更適合起訴書生成之參考案例。

## 2.2 Motivation

The reviewed studies show that RAG is a useful foundation for legal text generation, but conventional semantic retrieval still retrieves references mainly as flat top-\(k\) similar cases. As illustrated in Fig. 2.2(a), TAARN-style retrieval improves this setting by using a generic case graph, random walks, graph/text paths, and graph/text reasoning. This design can provide stronger relevance modeling than pure vector retrieval, but it still mainly retrieves top-\(k\) relevant cases according to general graph/text relations. It does not explicitly determine whether the retrieved cases are lighter, heavier, or structurally suitable for the current traffic-accident dispute.

綜合前述文獻可知，RAG 是法律文本生成的重要基礎，但傳統語意檢索仍多半將檢索結果視為平面的 top-\(k\) 相似案例。如 Fig. 2.2(a) 所示，TAARN-style retrieval 透過一般案件圖、random walks、graph/text paths 與 graph/text reasoning 改善此設定。此設計比純向量檢索具有更強的關聯性建模能力，但其主要仍是依一般 graph/text relations 取得 top-\(k\) relevant cases，並未明確判斷檢索案例相對於當前交通事故案件是較輕、較重，或是否在結構上適合作為參考。

The related work on graph-enhanced retrieval suggests that structural information can improve retrieval, but the graph itself must be designed according to the downstream task. A generic case graph can connect cases through shared laws, shared injuries, shared compensation items, or shared accident features. However, civil complaint generation also needs to know how the query case is positioned in terms of accident facts, injury severity, and compensation level. This is why the retrieval structure should not only find related cases, but also organize references around the query with severity-aware context.

結合圖譜之檢索研究說明，結構資訊有助於改善檢索，但圖譜本身必須依下游任務設計。一般案件圖可以透過共同法條、共同傷勢、共同賠償項目或共同事故特徵連結案件。然而，對民事起訴書生成而言，系統還需要知道 query 案件在事實、傷勢與賠償程度上的相對位置。因此，檢索結構不應只找相關案例，還需要圍繞 query 組織具嚴重度脈絡的參考案例。

This distinction is important because relevance and usefulness are not identical in complaint drafting. A case may be relevant because it involves the same injury type or compensation item, but it may still be unsuitable if the injury severity or compensation level is far from the query case. Conversely, a case may use different surface wording but still provide a useful drafting reference if its severity and legal structure are comparable. Therefore, the motivation is not to replace graph retrieval with a simpler structure, but to refine graph-based retrieval so that the retrieved references are organized according to the legal comparison needed by the drafting task.

此差異之所以重要，是因為在起訴書撰寫中，相關性與可用性並不完全相同。某案例可能因具有相同傷勢類型或賠償項目而相關，但若其傷勢嚴重度或賠償程度與 query 差距過大，仍未必適合作為撰寫參考。相反地，某案例雖然表面用語不同，但若其嚴重度與法律結構可比較，仍可能提供有用的書狀撰寫參考。因此，本章之動機並非以較簡單結構取代圖譜檢索，而是進一步調整 graph-based retrieval，使檢索參考能依書狀撰寫所需之法律比較關係被組織。

Based on this motivation, the proposed SDKG scheme transforms retrieval from generic relevance modeling into anchor-centered, severity-directional reference organization. As shown in Fig. 2.2(b), the lawyer input is first encoded through case feature extraction and mapped to an anchor case. The heavy-light tree retrieves lighter references, and the light-heavy tree retrieves heavier references. The retrieval budget is organized around these two directions, so the final top-\(k\) references can include both lighter-side and heavier-side comparable cases. In a balanced setting, the two directions may each contribute up to \(k/2\) references, while the implemented retrieval can also use the available candidates from both trees and rank them under the top-\(k\) budget. This design does not claim that trees are always better than graphs; rather, it uses two explicit severity directions to provide more useful drafting references for traffic-accident civil complaint generation.

基於此動機，所提出之 SDKG scheme 將檢索從一般關聯性建模，轉換為以 anchor case 為中心、具嚴重度方向之參考組織。如 Fig. 2.2(b) 所示，律師輸入會先經由 case feature extraction 編碼，接著映射至 anchor case。heavy-light tree 取得較輕參考案例，light-heavy tree 取得較重參考案例。檢索數量會圍繞兩個方向配置，使最終 top-\(k\) 參考可同時包含較輕側與較重側之可比較案例。在平衡設定下，兩個方向可各提供至多 \(k/2\) 筆參考；實作時則可由兩棵樹的可用候選案例中，在 top-\(k\) 預算內排序選取。此設計並非主張 tree 一定優於 graph，而是透過兩個明確嚴重度方向，為交通事故民事起訴書生成提供更適合撰寫使用的參考案例。

In other words, TAARN-style retrieval answers the question of which cases are generally relevant through graph/text paths, while the proposed SDKG scheme further asks how those comparable cases should be positioned around the anchor case. The light-heavy tree provides heavier-side references that can support upper-bound or more serious compensation contexts, whereas the heavy-light tree provides lighter-side references that can support lower-bound or less serious comparison contexts. By considering candidates from both directions under the top-\(k\) budget, the retrieved set avoids being dominated by only one severity side and provides a more balanced set of structured references for the generator.

換言之，TAARN-style retrieval 回答的是哪些案例可透過 graph/text paths 被判定為一般相關；所提出之 SDKG scheme 則進一步回答這些可比較案例應如何圍繞 anchor case 被定位。light-heavy tree 提供較重側參考案例，可支援較高嚴重度或較高賠償脈絡；heavy-light tree 提供較輕側參考案例，可支援較低嚴重度或較低賠償脈絡。透過在 top-\(k\) 預算內同時考量兩個方向之候選案例，檢索結果可避免被單一嚴重度側主導，並為生成模型提供較平衡的結構化參考集合。
