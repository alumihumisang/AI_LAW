# Chapter 7 Conclusions

This thesis proposed a Severity-Aware Dual-Knowledge-Graph (SDKG) scheme for traffic-accident civil complaint generation. The proposed scheme targets the limitation of conventional RAG, where retrieved cases are often selected mainly by semantic similarity and may not provide controlled severity-side references for legal drafting. This thesis first used the sentence-level-aware dual retrieval method as the Baseline Method, where paragraph and sentence summaries provide local drafting references. SDKG then extends this idea from paragraph and sentence retrieval to case-level comparable retrieval. To achieve this goal, SDKG converts traffic-accident civil complaint-style cases into structured case representations, compares cases pairwise under legal-feature distance and severity distance, and builds case relations only when the compared cases are sufficiently close under the threshold setting. The resulting comparable references are organized through a light-heavy tree and a heavy-light tree. At query time, the lawyer input is mapped to the closest existing case as the anchor case, and the corresponding node is used as the starting point for retrieving references from both heavier and lighter severity directions. The retrieved references, the anchor case, and drafting instructions are then assembled into a structured prompt for complaint generation.

The experimental design used simulated lawyer queries and human-written complaints prepared by legal-domain professionals as reference answers. The generated complaints are evaluated using BERTScore, BLEU, and ROUGE-L to measure semantic similarity, lexical overlap, and sequence overlap. The proposed evaluation setting provides a quantitative basis for comparing different SDKG configurations and examining how the severity weights, the distance-weight setting, the threshold setting, the number of retrieved references, and the number of available cases affect complaint generation quality. In the current automatic-evaluation results, the Baseline Method is higher than TAARN but lower than all SDKG configurations, showing that paragraph and sentence summaries are useful but still weaker than severity-aware case-level retrieval. The best observed SDKG setting occurs at top-8 with \(\alpha=0.5\), \(\beta=0.2\), \(\lambda=0.5\), and \(\tau=0.5\). Compared with TAARN, this setting improves BERTScore by 1.65 percentage points, BLEU by 6.71 percentage points, and ROUGE-L by 6.17 percentage points, corresponding to relative gains of 1.85%, 11.35%, and 8.67%, respectively. These results indicate that the proposed tree-guided retrieval does not merely increase the number of references, but provides more comparable legal and severity-side references for the generation model.

Overall, the proposed SDKG scheme organizes complete legal cases by legal structure and severity direction, allowing the generation model to use comparable references without directly copying unrelated case facts. The current results also show that the retrieval effect changes with top-\(k\), case-count coverage, and parameter settings, suggesting that SDKG should be evaluated as a configurable retrieval framework rather than a single fixed prompt strategy. Future work may extend the evaluation with larger test sets, completed legal-domain human scoring, additional case types, and more detailed analysis of how retrieved heavier-side and lighter-side references influence compensation drafting.

本論文提出一種用於交通事故民事起訴書生成之嚴重度感知雙知識圖譜方案（Severity-Aware Dual-Knowledge-Graph, SDKG）。所提出方案針對傳統 RAG 多以語意相似度選取案例之限制，解決檢索案例未必能提供法律撰寫所需之嚴重度方向參考的問題。本文先以 sentence-level-aware dual retrieval method 作為 Baseline Method，透過段落與句子摘要提供局部撰寫參考；SDKG 則進一步將此想法由段落與句子檢索延伸至案件層級可比較檢索。為此，所提出 SDKG scheme 將交通事故民事起訴書樣式案件轉換為結構化案件表示，並在法律特徵距離與嚴重度距離下進行兩兩案件比較；只有當案件距離落在門檻設定內且具備可比較性時，才建立案件關係。接著，系統透過 light-heavy tree 與 heavy-light tree 組織可比較參考案例。於查詢階段，系統會將律師輸入映射至最接近的既有案件作為錨點案件，並以其對應節點作為起始點，同時由較重與較輕兩個嚴重度方向取得參考案例。最後，檢索參考、錨點案件與撰寫指令會被組裝為結構化提示，以支援起訴書生成。

實驗設計使用模擬律師查詢，並以法律專業人員撰寫之人工起訴書作為參考答案。生成起訴書以 BERTScore、BLEU 與 ROUGE-L 進行評估，分別觀察語意相似度、文字片段重疊程度與序列重疊程度。此評估設定提供量化基礎，用以比較不同 SDKG 配置，並觀察嚴重度權重、距離權重、門檻設定、檢索案例數量與可用案件數對起訴書生成品質之影響。於目前自動化評估結果中，Baseline Method 高於 TAARN，但低於所有 SDKG 配置，表示段落與句子摘要檢索具有幫助，但仍不及嚴重度感知案件層級檢索。最佳 SDKG 設定出現在 top-8，參數為 \(\alpha=0.5\)、\(\beta=0.2\)、\(\lambda=0.5\)、\(\tau=0.5\)。相較於 TAARN，此設定在 BERTScore 提升 1.65 個百分點、BLEU 提升 6.71 個百分點、ROUGE-L 提升 6.17 個百分點；若以相對提升幅度觀察，分別約為 1.85%、11.35% 與 8.67%。此結果顯示，所提出方法並非單純增加參考案例數量，而是透過樹狀檢索提供更具法律結構與嚴重度可比較性的案例，使生成模型能取得更適合起訴書撰寫的參考內容。

整體而言，所提出 SDKG scheme 以法律結構與嚴重度方向組織完整案件，使生成模型能使用可比較參考，而非直接複製不相關案件事實。實驗結果亦顯示，檢索效果會受到 top-\(k\)、案件覆蓋量與參數設定影響，因此 SDKG 應被視為可調整之檢索框架，而非單一固定提示策略。未來研究可進一步擴大測試集、完成法律專業人工評分、延伸至更多案件類型，並更細緻分析較重側與較輕側參考對損害賠償撰寫之影響。
