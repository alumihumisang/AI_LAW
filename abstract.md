Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assists large language models in generating text by retrieving relevant external documents. However, when applied to Taiwanese traffic-accident civil complaint generation, conventional RAG mainly retrieves cases through semantic similarity and cannot explicitly organize accident facts, injury conditions, compensation items, and legal references for downstream drafting. As a result, the generated complaint may lose query focus, borrow unsuitable details from retrieved cases, or fail to preserve the structure required in legal pleadings. To address this issue, the proposed Severity-Aware Dual-Knowledge-Graph (SDKG) scheme constructs dual knowledge graphs through a light-heavy tree and a heavy-light tree, thereby supporting traffic-accident civil complaint generation through severity-ordered case relations and two severity-directional retrieval paths. The proposed scheme first converts 6,057 Taiwanese traffic-accident civil cases into fixed boolean case profiles covering four dimensions: litigants, fact, injury, and compensation. The fixed boolean case profiles are then used to reconstruct severity scores for the fact, injury, and compensation dimensions. Based on different experimental settings, legal-domain weights are assigned to the three severity dimensions, and distance thresholds are used to construct directed acyclic severity-aware DKG configurations. At query time, the system maps the input to the nearest anchor case, and the light-heavy tree and heavy-light tree retrieve heavier-side and lighter-side neighboring cases according to severity direction. Instead of directly feeding full retrieved cases into the language model, the SDKG scheme extracts structured references and drafting cues, which are then integrated into a segmented generation pipeline covering facts, legal provisions, damages, and conclusions. The evaluation builds 54 configurations from six severity-weight settings, three thresholds, and three distance weights. Preliminary validation is conducted using 50 test queries that simulate attorney inputs, with manually drafted complaints written by law school professionals as references. Compared with the TAARN-style competing method, the proposed SDKG scheme is expected to improve generation quality by approximately 7.3% in the final evaluation, indicating potential for controlled civil complaint generation in traffic-accident litigation.

Keywords: traffic accident, civil complaint generation, legal generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, severity-aware dual knowledge graph, dual-tree retrieval

檢索增強生成（Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RAG）是一種透過外部檢索結果輔助大型語言模型生成文本之技術。然而，對於臺灣交通事故民事起訴書生成而言，傳統 RAG 多半僅能依語意相似度擷取相似案例，卻難以明確組織事故事實、傷勢情形、賠償項目與法律依據，使生成結果容易偏離 query 主體、借用不適切案例細節，或無法穩定維持法律書狀所需之結構。為此，所提出之 Severity-Aware Dual-Knowledge-Graph（SDKG）scheme 透過 light-heavy tree 與 heavy-light tree 建構 dual knowledge graphs，並藉由嚴重度有序之案件關係與兩條嚴重度方向檢索路徑，支援交通事故民事起訴書之有效生成。所提出之 SDKG scheme 首先將 6,057 筆臺灣交通事故民事案件轉換為涵蓋 Litigants、Fact、Injury 與 Compensation 四個面向之固定 boolean case profiles，並進一步重建 Fact、Injury 與 Compensation 三項 severity scores。接著，系統依不同實驗設定，對三項 severity dimensions 指派法律領域權重，並透過距離閾值建立不具 directed cycles 之 severity-aware DKG configurations。於 query 階段，系統先將輸入映射至最近之 anchor case，再由 light-heavy tree 與 heavy-light tree 依嚴重度方向分別擷取較重側與較輕側鄰近案例。檢索所得案例並非直接以全文輸入大型語言模型，而是先轉換為結構化參考與書狀撰寫提示，再接入事實、法條、損害賠償與結論之分段式生成流程。評估共建立 54 組設定，由六種嚴重度權重、三種閾值與三種距離權重組成，並以 50 筆模擬律師輸入之測試 query，搭配由法律系專業人員撰寫之人工起訴書作為參考進行初步驗證。相較於 TAARN-style competing method，所提出之 SDKG scheme 預期可於最終評估中提升約 7.3% 之生成品質，顯示交通事故民事起訴書受控生成之應用潛力。
